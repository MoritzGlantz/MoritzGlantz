#### Some things I made

- [Contrast-Ratio.org](https://contrast-ratio.org/) – check the contrast ratio of text and background colors
- [Accessibility Cheatsheet](https://moritzglantz.de/accessibility-cheatsheet/) – a short guide to web accessibility best practices
- [Text Spacing Bookmarklet](https://github.com/MoritzGlantz/text-spacing-bookmarklet) – test WCAG 1.4.12 with one slider per property
- [Compat Matrix](https://moritzglantz.de/compatmatrix/) – compare browser feature support
- [Checkbox Tetris](https://moritzglantz.de/checkbox-tetris/) – Tetris, made of checkboxes

#### Apps for Mac and Apple TV

- [Audio Chain](https://moritzglantz.de/apps/audio-chain/) – live EQ, compression and Audio Unit effects for your microphone on Mac
- [LeanSpeak](https://moritzglantz.de/apps/leanspeak/) – a lean TeamSpeak 3 client for Mac
- [Streamicorn](https://moritzglantz.de/apps/streamicorn/) – PeerTube on your Apple TV
- [KoaxTV](https://moritzglantz.de/apps/koaxtv/) – cable TV and radio from your SAT>IP router on your Apple TV
